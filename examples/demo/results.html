Where this actually works

Nobody publishes where direct WebRTC connects — not per country, not per carrier, not per browser pair. This is the attempt: every row is one measured attempt, reviewed by the person who made it before it was published.

This page connects to a relay to read the public database — that is the whole of what it does, and it writes nothing. The demo itself still connects to nothing until you press publish.

What this cannot tell you yet

A dataset that overstates its coverage is worth less than one that says plainly what is missing. Each line below is a question nobody can answer from these rows — and an invitation, because the way to close one is to run it.
